Call'em turns your phone into a service bell for your home, villa, restaurant or office. Set up buttons like "Coffee", "Help" or "Come to the gate", choose who receives each one, and from then on a single tap sends a push notification or a Telegram message — no typing, no calls, no group chats.

How it works

1. Pick a preset

Home, villa, restaurant, care or office — and keep only the buttons you need.

2. Add people

Each person installs the free ntfy app and scans a QR code, or joins your Telegram bot with one link.

3. Tap

Buttons live on the home screen or in a widget. Important ones ask for a hold or a confirmation so nothing goes out by accident.

4. Rooms (optional)

Calibrate rooms by Wi‑Fi once and every message says where you are: "📍 Pool".

Frequently asked questions

Do the recipients need Call'em too?

No. They only need the ntfy app (Android, iOS or web) or Telegram. Call'em is for the person who presses the buttons.

Is it free?

Yes. The free version includes one place and up to two people. Pro is a one‑time purchase that unlocks unlimited places and people. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

Does it work without internet?

Messages need an internet connection on both phones (Wi‑Fi or mobile data). If your phone is offline, the call is queued and sent automatically as soon as the connection is back.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Call'em has no account and no server of its own; messages travel through ntfy.sh or Telegram. Details are in the privacy policy.

A message didn't arrive

Check that the recipient's ntfy app is subscribed to the topic (rescan the QR code from People) and that notifications for ntfy are allowed and not silenced by battery optimisation. For Telegram, make sure the person has pressed Start in your bot.

Why does the app ask for location?

Only when you set up Rooms: Android requires location permission to read nearby Wi‑Fi networks. The app never uses GPS and never sends location or Wi‑Fi data anywhere. If you skip rooms, the permission is never requested.

How do I delete my data?

Clear the call history in Settings → General, delete places and people in Settings, or simply uninstall the app — everything is stored locally.
