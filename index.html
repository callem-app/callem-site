Call'em turns your phone into a service bell for your home, villa, restaurant or office. Set up buttons like "Coffee", "Help" or "Come to the gate", choose who receives each one, and from then on a single tap sends the call to their phones — no typing, no phone calls, no group chats.

How it works

1. Pick a preset

Home, villa, restaurant, care or office — and keep only the buttons you need.

2. Add people

Each person installs the free Call'em app and scans your QR code once. No accounts, no phone numbers.

3. Tap

Buttons live on the home screen or in a widget. Important ones ask for a hold or a confirmation so nothing goes out by accident.

4. Rooms (optional)

Calibrate rooms by Wi‑Fi once and every message says where you are: "📍 Pool".

Frequently asked questions

Do the recipients need Call'em too?

Yes — the same free app, in "I receive alerts" mode. They scan your QR code once; calls arrive as a full‑screen alert with a sound, even on the lock screen, and they accept with one tap. You see who accepted.

Is it free?

Yes. The free version includes one place and up to two people. Pro is a one‑time purchase (€19.99) that unlocks unlimited places and people. There are no subscriptions and no ads. Where Google Play purchases are not available, we issue activation codes by email — see the Pro page.

Does it work without internet?

Messages need an internet connection on both phones (Wi‑Fi or mobile data). If your phone is offline, the call is queued and sent automatically as soon as the connection is back.

Where is my data?

On your phone. There is no account. Calls pass through our relay server end‑to‑end encrypted — it cannot read them and keeps only an encrypted copy for a short time. Details are in the privacy policy.

A message didn't arrive

Check that the person still appears under People on your phone and that Call'em on their phone is allowed to show notifications and is not restricted by battery optimisation. If they were removed or deleted the place, they need to scan your QR code again.

Why does the app ask for location?

Only when you set up Rooms: Android requires location permission to read nearby Wi‑Fi networks. The app never uses GPS and never sends location or Wi‑Fi data anywhere. If you skip rooms, the permission is never requested.

How do I delete my data?

Clear the call history in Settings → General, delete places and people in Settings, or simply uninstall the app — everything is stored locally.
