Privacy Policy

Call'em for Android, effective 21 October 2026

Call'em is a one-tap "call for help" button for your home, villa, restaurant or office. It sends short messages to people you choose. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you set up lives on your phone. Messages pass through our relay server in encrypted form — the server cannot read them.

In short

Stored on your phone

Places, buttons, people, rooms (Wi‑Fi fingerprints), settings, encryption keys and the list of your last 200 calls.

Sent when you tap a button

The message text, the place name and optionally the room name and time — delivered to the recipients you chose, end‑to‑end encrypted.

What our server sees

Random device identifiers, a push token and end‑to‑end encrypted messages. Never the text, names, or who you are.

Never collected

No analytics, crash reporters, advertising IDs, contacts, precise location, account or usage statistics.

3. What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device until you press a button or connect with someone. When you do, the app sends one message per recipient:

Our relay server

Recipients use the Call'em app too. Messages travel over HTTPS to our relay server at relay.callemapp.com, which forwards them to the recipient's phone through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging or, where that is unavailable, over a direct encrypted connection from the recipient's app.

Messages are end‑to‑end encrypted. The message text, the place name, the room name, the button name and the names of the people involved are encrypted on your phone with a key that only the owner's and the recipients' phones have (it is exchanged through the QR code, never through our server). The server stores and forwards ciphertext it cannot decrypt.

5. Analytics, advertising, tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, crash‑reporting or advertising SDKs and does not generate or use any advertising identifiers. The random device identifier used by the relay exists only to route your calls and is deleted together with the device record.

6. Purchases

Pro is an optional one‑time purchase. Where it is bought through Google Play, payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing; we receive no payment details. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where Google Play purchases are not available, Pro can be bought by e‑mail. In that case we receive what you send us: your e‑mail address, the phone code shown on the Pro screen (eight characters derived from the random device identifier; it does not identify you) and the payment confirmation. We keep this correspondence and a record of the sale (date, phone code, amount) for as long as tax law requires, use it only to issue the activation code and to help you if you change phones, and never share it. The activation code is checked and stored on your phone only; nothing about the purchase is sent to our server. It is included when you copy your setup to another phone of yours.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone.

8. Your choices

  • Clear history — Settings → General → Clear call history.
  • Remove a person or place — delete it in Settings. The data is removed from the device immediately; the connection on the relay is deleted and the person's app forgets the place.
  • Leave a place (as a recipient) — in Incoming alerts. The connection is removed from the relay and calls for that place can no longer be delivered to you.
  • Everything — uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. The relay keeps nothing that can identify you; remaining encrypted messages expire within the periods above, and the push token is cleared once it becomes invalid. If you would like the anonymous device record deleted as well, write to us and we will do it.
  • Messages already delivered stay in the recipient's Call'em app until they delete them.
