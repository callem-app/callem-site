Privacy Policy

Call'em for Android · Effective 19 October 2026

Call'em is a one-tap "call for help" button for your home, villa, restaurant or office. It sends short messages to people you choose. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. Everything you set up lives on your phone.

In short

Stored on your phone

Places, buttons, people, rooms (Wi‑Fi fingerprints), settings and the list of your last 200 calls.

Sent when you tap a button

The message text, the place name and optionally the room name and time — delivered through ntfy or Telegram to the recipients you chose.

Never collected

No analytics, crash reporters, advertising IDs, contacts, precise location or usage statistics. We have no server and receive nothing from you.

You are in control

Clear the call history or delete a place inside the app; uninstalling removes all data. Nothing is backed up to the cloud by the app.

3. What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device until you press a button. When you do, the app sends one message per recipient through the channel you configured:

ntfy (push notifications)

Messages are sent over HTTPS to ntfy.sh. A message contains: the place name, the button text, optionally the room name and time, and the topic of the recipient. Topics are long random identifiers generated on your phone. ntfy.sh is operated by a third party; see the ntfy privacy policy.

Telegram

If you choose Telegram, messages are sent to api.telegram.org using your own bot. The app also polls your bot for new chats so that people can join by tapping a link. Telegram's handling of messages is governed by the Telegram Privacy Policy.

In both cases we, the developer, are not in the path and do not receive, log or store any messages.

None. The app contains no analytics, crash‑reporting or advertising SDKs and does not generate or use any device or advertising identifiers.

6. Purchases

Optional in‑app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive no payment details. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone.

8. Your choices

  • Clear history — Settings → General → Clear call history.
  • Remove a person or place — delete it in Settings; the data is removed from the device immediately.
  • Everything — uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. Because the app has no server, there is nothing left to delete on our side.
  • Messages already delivered stay with the recipient's ntfy or Telegram app according to those services' rules.
