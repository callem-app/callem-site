The free version of Call'em includes one place and up to two people — enough for a small household. Pro removes both limits. It is a one‑time purchase: no subscription, no ads, no account.

What you get

Unlimited places

Villa, office, parents' home — each with its own buttons and people.

Unlimited people

Every button can reach as many people as you like.

Shared between your phones

Copy the setup to a second phone (Settings → Other phones) and Pro comes along with it.

One payment

€19.99 or the equivalent in your currency. Yours for good.

How to buy

Open Settings → Call'em Pro in the app. Where Google Play purchases are available, the Unlock button appears there and the purchase is handled by Google Play; use Restore purchase if you reinstall or change phones.

If the button is not shown, Google Play purchases are not available in your region yet. Write to support@callemapp.com with the code of this phone shown on the Pro screen (eight characters such as HVVX‑1HX3); we will arrange payment and reply with an activation code.

Activation codes

How do I enter the code?

Tap the link in our email on the phone where Call'em is installed — the app opens and confirms activation. Or open Settings → Call'em Pro → I have an activation code and paste the code.

The app says the code is for a different phone

Codes are tied to the phone code we signed. Open the Pro screen, tap the phone code to copy it and send it to us again — we will issue a new code free of charge.

I got a new phone

Set up the new phone with Copy from another phone (or Settings → Other phones on the old one): places, people and Pro move together. If the old phone is lost, write to us with the new phone code.

Does Pro need internet or an account?

No. The code is checked on the phone itself and is stored there. Nothing about the purchase is sent to our server.

Оплата в рублях

В России Pro приобретается напрямую у нас: 1 990 ₽, разовый платёж, без подписки.

  1. Откройте в приложении Настройки → Call'em Pro и нажмите «Написать нам» (или напишите на support@callemapp.com, указав код телефона с экрана Pro — восемь символов вида HVVX‑1HX3).

Код выпускается для телефона, код которого вы указали. При переносе настроек на другой телефон (Настройки → Другие телефоны) Pro переезжает вместе с ними. Если телефон потерян — напишите нам код нового, выпустим новый бесплатно.
